# Genesis 1-4 — One Video (66s)
Format: dark edit monologue, word-by-word yellow subtitles, red CTA

## The single thread across all 4 chapters
**God gave you everything from the start — identity, purpose, relationship, choice. And all man did was waste it.**

## Script (~190 words — 66s)

**[0-11s — HOOK]**
In four chapters, God gives man everything.
His identity, his purpose, his partner, and the right to choose.
And in four chapters, man wasted it all.

**[11-18s — NEGATION]**
You need to understand that Genesis 1 through 4 is not a history lesson.
It's not a science class, not a children's story, not a debate about an apple.

**[18-27s — REVELATION]**
These four chapters are a mirror.
Chapter 1: God makes you in His image — you are a creator.
Chapter 2: He gives you a mission before He gives you a relationship.
Chapter 3: you stay silent when you should speak, and you lose everything.
Chapter 4: you give your worst, you refuse to correct yourself, and you destroy what's beside you.

**[27-33s — SHOCK PROOF]**
Let me remind you that between chapter 1 and chapter 4, there are only three generations.
Three. God creates man perfect, and three generations later, a brother kills his brother.
So stop believing the problem is the world — the problem is you.

**[33-51s — INSIDER BREAKDOWN]**
If you look at the sequence closely, every fall comes from the same flaw.
In chapter 1, God speaks and creates. In chapter 3, Adam stays silent and destroys.
In chapter 2, God gives the best first. In chapter 4, Cain gives his leftovers.
The pattern is always the same: God acts with intention, and man acts out of laziness.
And that's exactly what you do — you received the image of God, His mission, His breath, and you live as if you had nothing.

**[51-58s — HARD NUMBER]**
God took six days to build everything.
And man took one single verse to lose it all — Genesis 3, verse 6.
One verse. How many bad decisions do you need?

**[58-66s — RECALL + CTA]**
Genesis 1 through 4: God gave you everything — identity, purpose, relationship, choice.
Stop wasting it. And act.
